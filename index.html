Innovating Medical Education with AI

Seeking the NHSE Clinical Fellowship to leverage artificial intelligence, reduce clinical drudgery, and build a future where technology augments medical expertise for better patient outcomes.

My Vision for AI in Medicine

This fellowship is an opportunity to translate frontline clinical insights into tangible technological solutions. My vision is grounded in practical applications that support clinicians, enhance training, and improve patient safety by intelligently integrating AI into our daily workflows and educational pathways.

Automate and Assist

AI can automate repetitive, administrative tasks where fatigue leads to errors. This frees clinicians to focus on complex decision-making and patient care, acting as an invaluable assistant to speed up care and optimise treatment pathways.

Augment Expertise

This isn't about replacing clinicians, but augmenting them. I envision tools that act as a "second opinion" on a night shift or use narrow LLMs to provide rapid, evidence-based answers at the bedside, enhancing our capabilities when we need it most.

Shape Future Training

By working within NHSE, I aim to help shape how these powerful tools are integrated into postgraduate training from the ground up, ensuring the next generation of doctors can effectively collaborate with the AI that will define our future.

Proposed 6-Month Plan

My strategy is a dual-stream approach that grounds the fellowship's work in genuine user need while maintaining momentum on existing innovations. This method balances collaborative discovery with agile development to maximise the fellowship's potential impact from day one.

Months 1-3

Phase 1: Needs-Led Priority Setting

Conduct a comprehensive scoping exercise modelled on the James Lind Alliance principles. Through surveys, workshops, and focus groups with EM doctors, I will collaboratively produce a "top 10" list of clinician-identified priorities for AI in EM education. This ensures our work is relevant and adoptable.

Months 4-6

Phase 2: Dual-Stream Project Development

Stream A: Needs-Led Prototyping

Select a high-priority area identified by clinicians and begin designing a minimum viable product (MVP), ensuring the fellowship is immediately responsive to community needs.

Stream B: Personal Innovation Stream

Continue development of my existing projects, particularly the AI-driven simulation generator and triage support tool, to build on promising work and maintain a pipeline of innovation.

End of 6 Months

Key Outcomes

This approach will yield a user-validated roadmap for future projects and tangible progress on both a new, needs-led prototype and my existing portfolio of applications.
